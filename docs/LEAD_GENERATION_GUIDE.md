## 🎁 **PHASE 4: Lead Capture Mechanisms** (Week 3)

### Implemented Features

#### 1. **Exit-Intent Popup** ✅
- Triggers when user about to leave
- Offers free guide + consultation
- 15-25% conversion rate typical

#### 2. **Quick Contact Modal** ✅
- Appears on "Book now" clicks
- Compact 3-field form
- Integrated with chatbot

#### 3. **Chatbot Lead Capture** ✅
- Quick contact button in header
- Captures leads during conversations
- Automated follow-up

#### 4. **Scroll-Triggered Popup**
- Appears at 50% scroll depth
- Offers relevant resource
- Non-intrusive timing

#### 5. **Timed Popup**
- Appears after 30 seconds
- For engaged visitors
- Different offer than exit-intent
